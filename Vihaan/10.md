## 📌 1. ScrollTrigger Section Pinning
Pin the `#repo-city` viewport section to frame the 3D city as the user scrolls:
```javascript
ScrollTrigger.create({
  trigger: "#repo-city",
  start: "top top",
  end: "+=120%",
  pin: true,
  scrub: false, // Invariant: Entry wave is NOT scrubbed
});
```
- The section pins firmly at the viewport top for `120%` scroll travel, allowing the user to observe the entry wave, explore tooltips, and click flagged buildings before the page unpins and advances.

## 🧹 3. Resource Teardown & Lifecycle (`destroy`)
Implement a comprehensive cleanup function `destroy()` attached to `window.addEventListener('beforeunload', destroy)`:
1. **Kill ScrollTriggers:**
   `ScrollTrigger.getAll().forEach(st => st.kill());`
2. **Disconnect Observers:**
   - Disconnect `ResizeObserver`.
   - Disconnect `IntersectionObserver`.
3. **Remove DOM Event Listeners:**
   - Remove `pointermove`, `click`, and `resize` handlers from `window` and `#city-container`.
4. **Dispose WebGL Geometries & Materials:**
   - Traverse `scene` and call `.dispose()` on all mesh geometries, line geometries, standard materials, basic materials, and textures.
5. **Clear Arc Resources:**
   - Remove active laser arc lines and dispose their buffer geometries.
6. **Dispose Renderer & Canvas:**
   - Call `renderer.dispose()`.
   - Remove `renderer.domElement` from `#city-container`.
